## Privacy Policy for Mama Eats

**Last updated:** May 12, 2026

Mama Eats is a mobile application dedicated to nutritional information and support for pregnant women. This Privacy Policy explains what data we may collect, how we use it, and what choices you have as a user.

## 1. Data Controller (Responsable del Tratamiento de los Datos)

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application **Mama Eats: pregnancy-safe food** (hereinafter, the "Application").

The data controller responsible for the processing of personal data collected through the Application is **Unarenque** (developer account holder), operating commercially under the brand name **Propeller Apps** (hereinafter, the "Developer").

Any reference in this document to "we", "us", "our", or "the Developer" refers jointly to Unarenque and Propeller Apps.

*   **Application:** Mama Eats: pregnancy-safe food
*   **Privacy, Support, and Inquiries Contact:** mama.eats.pregnant.food@gmail.com

### 2. What data we may collect

Mama Eats may process the following types of data:

- Technical device data, such as model, operating system, language, and app version.
- Usage data, such as screens visited, interactions, and internal events.
- Device advertising identifiers, when available and permitted by the user.
- Information necessary to display ads and measure their performance.
- Information that the user voluntarily enters in the app, for example searches or preferences within Mama Eats.

We do not request, unless expressly indicated, identifying data such as name, address, or phone number to use the basic app.

### 3. What we use the data for

We use the data to:

- Make the app work correctly.
- Display Mama Eats content and features.
- Display ads.
- Measure ad performance and frequency.
- Improve user experience.
- Detect errors, abuse, or technical problems.
- Manage privacy and consent preferences.

### 4. Advertising

Mama Eats displays ads through third-party advertising providers.

Providers that may participate in ad delivery include:

- **Google AdMob**
- **Huawei Ads**
- **Amazon Ads**

These providers may use device identifiers, usage data, and context signals to display ads, measure their performance, and, when the user permits, personalize advertising.

Depending on the user's settings and applicable regulations, advertising may be:

- personalized,
- non-personalized,
- or limited.

### 5. Consent

When required by regulations, Mama Eats requests consent for the processing of data related to advertising and personalization through a consent management system.  
The user can accept, reject, or modify their preferences when the application permits.

If the user does not consent to personalization, the app will continue to function and may display non-personalized or limited ads when possible.

### 6. Legal basis

Depending on the country and the specific processing, the legal basis may be:

- Your consent.
- The need to provide the service.
- Legitimate interest, when applicable and compatible with regulations.
- Compliance with legal obligations.

### 7. Sharing data with third parties

We may share data with technology and advertising providers that provide services to Mama Eats, exclusively for the purposes described in this policy.

These third parties may act as independent controllers or processors, depending on the case and their own privacy policy.

We recommend you also review the privacy policies of:

- Google
- Huawei
- Amazon

### 8. Data retention

We retain data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, while the app is in use, or while necessary for legal, technical, or security reasons.

### 9. Your rights

Depending on your country and applicable regulations, you may have the right to:

- Access your data.
- Rectify incorrect data.
- Request its deletion.
- Limit its processing.
- Object to processing.
- Withdraw consent at any time.
- Request portability, when applicable.

Account and Data Deletion Request:

If you wish to delete your Mama Eats account and all associated data, please send us an email at mama.eats.pregnant.food@gmail.com with your request, and we will process it within 72 hours.

To exercise your rights, write to: mama.eats.pregnant.food@gmail.com

### 10. Minors

Mama Eats is not directed to minors if local regulations require a minimum age for data processing or personalized advertising.  
If we detect that a minor has provided data improperly, we will attempt to delete it when reasonably possible.

### 11. Security

We apply reasonable measures to protect data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or improper disclosure. No system is completely secure, but we try to reduce risks on an ongoing basis.

### 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, legislation, or providers used change.  
The current version will always be the one published within the app or at the indicated URL.

### 13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact:

**Email:** mama.eats.pregnant.food@gmail.com
