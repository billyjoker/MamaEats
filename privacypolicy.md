# Privacy Policy for Mama Eats

**Last updated:** May 12, 2026

Mama Eats is a mobile application dedicated to nutritional information and support for pregnant women. This Privacy Policy explains what data we may collect, how we use it, and what choices you have as a user.

## 1. Data Controller

The controller responsible for this application is:

**Mama Eats**  
**Email:** mama.eats.pregnant.food@gmail.com

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can write to: mama.eats.pregnant.food@gmail.com

## 2. What data we may collect

Mama Eats may process the following types of data:

- Technical device data, such as model, operating system, language, and app version.
- Usage data, such as screens visited, interactions, and internal events.
- Device advertising identifiers, when available and permitted by the user.
- Information necessary to display ads and measure their performance.
- Information that the user voluntarily enters in the app, for example searches or preferences within Mama Eats.

We do not request, unless expressly indicated, identifying data such as name, address, or phone number to use the basic app.

## 3. What we use the data for

We use the data to:

- Make the app work correctly.
- Display Mama Eats content and features.
- Display ads.
- Measure ad performance and frequency.
- Improve user experience.
- Detect errors, abuse, or technical problems.
- Manage privacy and consent preferences.

Mama Eats displays ads through third-party advertising providers.

Providers that may participate in ad delivery include:

- **Google AdMob**
- **Huawei Ads**

These providers may use device identifiers, usage data, and context signals to display ads, measure their performance, and, when the user permits, personalize advertising.

Depending on the user's settings and applicable regulations, advertising may be:

- personalized,
- non-personalized,
- or limited.

## 5. Consent

When required by regulations, Mama Eats requests consent for the processing of data related to advertising and personalization through a consent management system.  
The user can accept, reject, or modify their preferences when the application permits.

If the user does not consent to personalization, the app will continue to function and may display non-personalized or limited ads when possible.

## 6. Legal basis

Depending on the country and the specific processing, the legal basis may be:

- Your consent.
